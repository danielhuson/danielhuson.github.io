Profile

I am Professor of Algorithms in Bioinformatics at the University of Tübingen, working on algorithms and software systems for computational biology.

My work spans phylogenetic trees and networks, metagenomic analysis, sequence bioinformatics, scientific visualization, and the design of robust, interactive software systems. I have developed and contributed to widely used tools including MEGAN, SplitsTree, Dendroscope, PhyloSketchApp, and related platforms.

A recurring theme in my work is the translation of mathematical and algorithmic ideas into usable software for scientists: systems that are not only correct and efficient, but also accessible, visual, maintainable, and distributable across platforms.

Professional interests

  • Phylogenetic trees, networks, and visualization
  • Metagenomic analysis and microbiome software
  • Scientific software engineering and research software productization
  • Advanced Java, JavaFX, cross-platform deployment, and mobile scientific applications
  • Teaching algorithms and scientific programming
