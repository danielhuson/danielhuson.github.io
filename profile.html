Short CV

Daniel Huson is Professor of Algorithms in Bioinformatics at the University of Tübingen, Germany. His work focuses on algorithms, visualization systems, phylogenetic networks, metagenomics, and scientific software engineering.

He studied mathematics at Bielefeld University, where he completed both his doctorate and habilitation under Andreas Dress.

Before joining the University of Tübingen in 2002, he worked as Senior Staff Scientist at Celera Genomics. He has also held visiting professorships at Nanyang Technological University and the National University of Singapore, and was awarded a Royal Society of New Zealand International Catalyst Fellowship.

He is the developer of software systems including MEGAN, SplitsTree, Dendroscope, and PhyloSketchApp, and has supervised over 100 BSc and MSc theses and participated in the supervision of over 60 PhD theses.
