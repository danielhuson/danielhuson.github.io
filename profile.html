Profile

Daniel H. Huson is Professor of Algorithms in Bioinformatics at the University of Tübingen, Germany. His work focuses on algorithms, phylogenetic networks, metagenomics, visualization systems, and scientific software engineering.

Short CV

  • Professor of Algorithms in Bioinformatics, University of Tübingen, since 2002
  • Royal Society of New Zealand International Catalyst Fellow, 2020–2024
  • Visiting Professor, National University of Singapore, 2015–2020
  • Visiting Professor, Nanyang Technological University, 2011–2015
  • Senior Staff Scientist, Celera Genomics, 1999–2002
  • Post-Doc UPenn and Princeton University, 1997-1999
  • Doctorate and Habilitation in mathematics, Bielefeld University, 1990-1997
