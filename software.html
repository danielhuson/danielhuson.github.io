Selected software

Software systems for computational biology, phylogenetics, metagenomics, visualization, and scientific computing.

MEGAN

Interactive analysis and visualization of metagenomic data. Current work includes MEGAN 7 and concepts for a modernized MEGAN 8 ecosystem.

SplitsTree

Software for phylogenetic trees, split networks, and related methods for evolutionary analysis.

Dendroscope

Interactive visualization and analysis of large phylogenetic trees and networks.

PhyloSketchApp

Touch-based creation and exploration of phylogenetic trees and networks, including mobile/iOS deployment.

CatReNet

Exploration of catalytic and autocatalytic reaction networks using mathematical and algorithmic methods.

JPICL

A JavaFX-based interface and cross-platform distribution workflow for PICL, a C program by Laura Kubatko for phylogenetic inference.
